What's new in the AE Innovation Hub prototype3

V2 · under the pages

Feeds, workflows and signed changes

Two feeds

Pages never talk to Odoo. They read a public feed (published records only) and a private feed, encrypted with AES-256-GCM and opened in the browser with the team's access key.

Workflows: 10 agentic cells

The small requests that arrive ten at a time, each mapped end to end: who to talk to, mentor requests, membership, monthly KPIs, the weekly digest. Nothing is sent; a person approves each step.

Plan, then apply

Every proposed change is queued, checked against a written policy, signed by a person (ECDSA P-256) and applied by a broker that keeps a record. It writes only to our experimentation Odoo.

Briefing for Algarve Evolution's Odoo

The 14-slide briefing "AE Innovation Hub and Odoo: how we work" explains this approach to the person who manages their Odoo. It is in the decks section beside this one.

What's new in the AE Innovation Hub prototype4

V3 · why

A version with no made-up data at all

  • António liked four of the V1 designs: The Brief, Mission Control, The Atlas and The Future.
  • V2's simulated records are useful but easy to mistake. V3 shows only what Algarve Evolution has published, so every number can be checked.
  • The rule, written into the feed: "Real, published data only. Every record carries its source URL; nothing is simulated. No person is named except António Guerreiro, executive director."
  • Gaps are shown, not filled. Where a record does not exist in public, the page says "your Odoo would fill this", names the Odoo models that would hold it and links to the V2 page that shows the idea.

What's new in the AE Innovation Hub prototype10

Our pick

The Brief as the front door; the others as views

  • It is the easiest to check, which is the point of V3: every fact has a numbered note.
  • The gaps are plainest: each activity says what is published and what only their Odoo can add.
  • It works everywhere: phones, print, dark mode, no motion needed, the lightest of the four.
  • It is closest to what António asked for: four activities on one Odoo, told in two minutes.
  • Keep the others as views of the same feed: The Atlas for the region, Mission Control on a screen at events, The Future for a talk.

A recommendation, not a decision: António and Algarve Evolution choose.
