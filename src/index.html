Algarve, Portugal

Algarve Evolution

A movement that began in 2018 as a small group of local entrepreneurs, working to make the Algarve a southern European innovation hub with an economy beyond tourism. It works on three pillars: education, startups and smaller companies, and the ecosystem itself.

Sources: SGit Newsroom, PrompTugal.news
