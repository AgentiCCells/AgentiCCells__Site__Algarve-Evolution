Our talent map

Who in the Algarve can help you, by town and by need.

Pay per use, after the value: every action here costs a few cents of simulated credit (opening the page 1 cent, a search 2, a profile 3, a contact 50). Your credit is in the corner. Nothing is charged.

We have mapped the people in the region who provide services, mentor and invest. This prototype shows how that map could work in public: say what you need, and find the organisations and people who fit.

The organisations are real, each with its public source. The people are simulated: in the real map, nobody is listed until they have seen their own listing and said yes.
