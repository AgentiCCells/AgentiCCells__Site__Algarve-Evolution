Faro, Algarve · since 2018

Building the Algarve's economy beyond tourism

We are Algarve Evolution, a Faro-based association that connects the region's entrepreneurs, talent, startups, smaller companies and partners. We began in 2018 as a small group of local entrepreneurs. Now we are making the ecosystem work.

04What we do

Our work, on three pillars

We work on three pillars: education, startups and smaller companies, and the innovation ecosystem that keeps people and companies in the region.

Swipe →

Education

  • Entrepreneurship programme with the University of the Algarve

    Students on the Penha, Gambelas and Portimão campuses develop and present ideas on the Lean Model Canvas.

    Source: SGit Newsroom

  • "The Company", with Junior Achievement Portugal

    Secondary-school students create and run a simulated company.

    Source: SGit Newsroom

  • Buildathons

    Days of building with AI, such as Vibathon Algarve 2026.

    Source: SGit Newsroom

Startups and smaller companies

  • Founder2Founder

    A six-month growth programme for founder-led companies with customers and revenue, launched in October 2026: eight companies chosen from 41 applications, each with a mentor and three measurable targets. It takes no equity.

    Sources: PrompTugal.news, SGit Newsroom

  • Incubator

    Recognised by Startup Portugal in January 2026. Early-stage incubation, from validation to first customers, is planned for 2027.

    Sources: PrompTugal.news, SGit Newsroom

  • INOVA Algarve 3.0

    A consortium programme helping the region's tourism businesses understand AI and robotics, with a pilot of free, personalised consultancy for 20 of them.

    Sources: PrompTugal.news, SGit Newsroom

  • Annual startup showcase

    Startups present to experienced professionals and investors.

    Source: SGit Newsroom

The ecosystem

  • Algarve Digital Innovators

    A network that has mapped 24 digital communities across the region's 16 municipalities, with a development plan to 2030 that depends on funding.

    Sources: PrompTugal.news, SGit Newsroom

  • Algarve Tech Hub Summit

    Co-organised with the University of the Algarve and Algarve STP.

    Source: PrompTugal.news

  • Digitalisation and ICT platform for CCDR Algarve

    Leading the platform that co-creates a regional collaborative plan.

    Source: SGit Newsroom
