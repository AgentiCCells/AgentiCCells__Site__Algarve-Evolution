Transmission from Algarve Evolution

Building the Algarve's economy beyond tourism

We are Algarve Evolution, a Faro-based association that connects the region's entrepreneurs, talent, startups, smaller companies and partners. We began in 2018 as a small group of local entrepreneurs. Now we are making the ecosystem work.

Mandate Our mandate is to diversify the Algarve's economy beyond tourism and help the region's established industries become more competitive.

Source: Portugal Startup News

What each programme does
  • Entrepreneurship programme with the University of the Algarve Students on the Penha, Gambelas and Portimão campuses develop and present ideas on the Lean Model Canvas.
  • "The Company", with Junior Achievement Portugal Secondary-school students create and run a simulated company.
  • Buildathons Days of building with AI, such as Vibathon Algarve 2026.
  • Founder2Founder A six-month growth programme for founder-led companies with customers and revenue, launched in October 2026: eight companies chosen from 41 applications, each with a mentor and three measurable targets. It takes no equity.
  • Incubator Recognised by Startup Portugal in January 2026. Early-stage incubation, from validation to first customers, is planned for 2027.
  • INOVA Algarve 3.0 A consortium programme helping the region's tourism businesses understand AI and robotics, with a pilot of free, personalised consultancy for 20 of them.
  • Annual startup showcase Startups present to experienced professionals and investors.
  • Algarve Digital Innovators A network that has mapped 24 digital communities across the region's 16 municipalities, with a development plan to 2030 that depends on funding.
  • Algarve Tech Hub Summit Co-organised with the University of the Algarve and Algarve STP.
  • Digitalisation and ICT platform for CCDR Algarve Leading the platform that co-creates a regional collaborative plan.
