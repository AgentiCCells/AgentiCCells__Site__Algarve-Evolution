# Closing the house (holiday home, Algarve): a simulated example

A Word document of the kind people keep for years. Invented content; nothing confidential. The important steps are in **bold**.

## The day before
- **Call the neighbour to say we are leaving tomorrow**
- Check the weather for the drive to the airport
- **Confirm the cleaner for Thursday**
- Empty the fridge; give the fresh food to the neighbour
- Take the bins out (collection is Monday and Thursday)

## On the day
- **Turn off the gas at the bottle**
- **Turn off the water heater**
- Turn off the water at the main tap if away more than two weeks
- Unplug the TV, router and microwave
- **Close all the shutters**
- Water the plants on the terrace
- Leave the key in the agreed place
- **Set the alarm and check the app shows it armed**
- **Lock both doors and check the gate**

## When we arrive back
- Turn the water back on before the water heater
- Open the shutters and air the house
- Check the fridge and the freezer
- Message the neighbour that we are back
